## Connecting Music Educators Globally

The International Society for Music Education (ISME) is the premier global organization representing music educators across all levels and cultures. Affiliated with the International Music Council and UNESCO, ISME fosters intercultural understanding, academic research, and international collaboration to ensure high-quality music education is accessible to everyone.

### Key Initiatives

- **World Conferences & Assemblies:** Hosts massive biennial global conferences that bring together thousands of scholars, teachers, performers, and researchers to share pedagogical practices and musical research.
- **Specialized Commissions:** Coordinates active international commissions focusing on:
  - **Early Childhood Music Education**
  - **Community Music Activity**
  - **Music in Special Education and Music Therapy**
  - **Research & Development in Music Education**
- **International Journal of Music Education (IJME):** Publishes a leading peer-reviewed academic journal featuring rigorous research on curriculum design, teacher training, and comparative music studies.
- **Global Advocacy Programs:** Campaigns internationally to defend arts education in national curriculums and support teachers in developing nations.
