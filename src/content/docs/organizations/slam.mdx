## Empowering Music Ensembles Through Selfless Leadership

The Servant Leadership Association for Music (SLAM) is dedicated to transforming school band, orchestra, and choral programs by developing high-character student leaders. Based on the philosophy of service leadership, SLAM provides school programs with training materials, workshop curriculum, and consulting to build a healthy, student-driven organizational culture.

### Key Initiatives

- **Student Leadership Training Curriculums:** Comprehensive, year-long curricula tailored to school ensembles, outlining how student officers can manage logistics, mentor younger peers, and build program pride.
- **On-Site & Virtual Leadership Workshops:** Interactive training sessions led by experienced music educators, helping student leadership teams establish clear goals, resolve conflicts, and practice effective communication.
- **Resource and Template Libraries:** Access reproducible documents, application forms, officer interview rubrics, and leadership evaluation tools to simplify program logistics.
- **Mindset & Character Development:** Focuses on helping students cultivate empathy, self-discipline, responsibility, and an active commitment to supporting their peers and directors.
