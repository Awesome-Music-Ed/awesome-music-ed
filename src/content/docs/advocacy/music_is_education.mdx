## Unifying Music Advocacy Efforts Nationwide

The Music IS Education Coalition compiles data-driven research and policy briefs to advocate for music education as a fundamental right for all students. By collaborating with arts organizations, research institutions, and community foundations, the coalition ensures music programs are supported by national educational policy.

### Core Advocacy Resources

- **Data-Driven Policy Briefs:** In-depth, scholarly papers analyzing how music enrollment correlates with student attendance, graduation rates, and long-term academic success.
- **Local Campaign Media Kits:** Professionally designed brochures, social media graphics, and press release templates that local coalitions can use to rally public support.
- **School District Audit Guides:** Step-by-step instructions for assessing equity and access in local school music programs to identify underserved student demographics.
- **State Policy Analysis Reports:** Tracks state-level graduation requirements and curriculum standards to ensure compliance with federal well-rounded education goals.
