## Raising the Standard of Musical Expressiveness

Written by the legendary Director of Bands at the University of Michigan, H. Robert Reynolds, *In Pursuit of Great Conducting* focuses heavily on the philosophical, aesthetic, and artistic responsibilities of a conductor. Rather than focusing solely on basic manual beat-patterns, Reynolds encourages conductors to search for the deeper emotional truths within a musical score.

### Core Pedagogical Themes

- **Artistic Intent Over Mechanical Accuracy:** Reynolds argues that while technical competence is essential, a great conductor must transcend basic time-keeping and communicate the profound emotional essence of the composer's score.
- **Deep Score Study as a Moral Obligation:** Explains how to live with a score, researching the historical context, harmonic architecture, and composer's life to uncover musical secrets that cannot be represented by printed ink alone.
- **Rehearsal Psychology and Leadership:** Advice on creating an atmosphere of collaborative music-making, where performers are respected and inspired to bring their own artistic passion to the ensemble.
- **The Integrity of the Conductor:** Stresses the importance of humility, continuous personal and artistic growth, and a lifelong dedication to musical excellence.
