## The Definitive Guide to Ensemble Intonation

Shelley Jagow's *Tuning for Wind Instruments* is the gold-standard reference manual for wind band directors seeking to master intonation. Packed with diagnostic charts, acoustic principles, and practical tuning procedures, this book demystifies pitch tendencies and provides a scientific, systematic approach to building a beautifully tuned band.

### Core Pedagogical Themes

- **Understanding Acoustic Principles:** Explains the science of sound waves, beat frequencies, equal temperament vs. just intonation, and how temperature, humidity, and room acoustics affect pitch.
- **Instrument-Specific Pitch Tendencies:** Meticulous analysis of each woodwind and brass instrument, detailing which registers are naturally sharp or flat and how to compensate using embouchure, voicing, or alternate fingerings.
- **Systematic Tuning Procedures:** Step-by-step instructions for tuning individuals and ensembles, including proper tuning note selection and how to utilize drones effectively in rehearsals.
- **Intonation Charts and Visual Aids:** Includes highly practical, reproducible fingering charts and pitch tendency worksheets for students to log and track their instrument's specific behaviors.
