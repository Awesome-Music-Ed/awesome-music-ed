Welcome to **Awesome Music Ed**! Whether you are a veteran music educator, a fresh-faced student teacher, or a private instructor, this site is designed to be your one-stop hub for the best resources in the profession.

## What is Awesome Music Ed?

Awesome Music Ed is a community-driven, open-source project dedicated to curating and organizing the best tools and information for music educators. Our mission is to provide a list of high-quality, curated resources for music educators of all ages.

## How to Explore

The project is organized into several key categories, which you can find in the sidebar:

- **Tools**: Software and apps for communication, program management, and classroom use.
- **Pedagogy**: Resources for curriculum development and instructional strategies.
- **Books**: A list of literature significant to music education and career growth.
- **Podcasts**: Audio resources for learning on the go.
- **Advocacy**: Tools and information to help you promote and defend your music program.
- **Organizations**: Links to state and national professional associations.

## Finding What You Need

1. **Use the Sidebar**: All categories are neatly nested to help you drill down into specific areas of study.
2. **Search**: Use the search bar at the top of the page (or press `Ctrl+K`) to quickly find a specific tool or organization.

## Get Involved

This list is only as good as the community that builds it! If you know of a resource that should be here—or if you find a link that needs updating—please let us know.

- **Learn how to contribute**: Read our [Contribution Guidelines](/contributions/).
- **Submit an issue**: Visit our [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Awesome-Music-Ed/awesome-music-ed/issues) page.
