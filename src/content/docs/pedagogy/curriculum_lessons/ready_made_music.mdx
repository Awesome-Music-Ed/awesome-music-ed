*From the Ready Made Music website:*

## Quick, High-Quality Music Lessons and Classroom Games

Ready Made Music is a digital resource store and curriculum hub built specifically for busy elementary general music teachers. Designed to alleviate prep time, the site provides creative, fully designed lesson units, active movement games, and visual aids that keep young learners engaged.

### Key Resources

- **Interactive Whiteboard Lessons:** Ready-to-teach slide decks (for Google Slides and PowerPoint) reviewing notation, rhythm, and composer biographies.
- **Active Music Games:** Fun, movement-based games that teach musical terminology, pitch matching, and rhythm subdivisions while keeping students physically active.
- **Sub Plans & Printables:** Easily downloadable worksheets, coloring pages, and emergency sub plans that require minimal preparation.
- **Seasonal & Themed Units:** Holiday and season-specific music packs to keep lessons culturally relevant and fresh throughout the school year.
