## Free Classroom Resources for Music Educators

The Music Educator’s Toolbox is an extensive online resource developed by Carnegie Hall’s Weill Music Institute. Designed for elementary and middle school general music classrooms, it provides grade-specific lesson plans, worksheets, audio resources, assessment rubrics, and video exemplars to support teachers.

### Key Resources

- **Grade-Specific Lesson Plans:** Access sequential, standard-aligned lesson plans for grades K–5, covering core musical concepts like rhythm, meter, pitch, tempo, and form.
- **Formative Assessment Rubrics:** Utilize ready-made rubrics and assessment models to evaluate student progress in vocal performance, instrument playing, and creative composition.
- **Instructional Media & Videos:** Watch professional music educators demonstrate strategies and lead active lessons in real-world classrooms.
- **Printable Student Worksheets:** Download high-quality PDF worksheets, graphic organizers, and notation templates ready for classroom distribution.
