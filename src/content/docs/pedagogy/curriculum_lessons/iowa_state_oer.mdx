## Free and Open-Access Music Resources

Curated by the Iowa State University Library, this comprehensive guide collects Open Educational Resources (OERs) for music instruction. OERs are free-to-use, openly licensed educational materials that can be customized and distributed by teachers without copyright restrictions, reducing the financial burden of purchasing textbooks.

### Key Resources

- **Open Music Theory Textbooks:** Links to comprehensive peer-reviewed music theory resources, covering fundamentals, chromatic harmony, and post-tonal analysis.
- **Open Score Databases:** Directories of free, public domain score libraries, including historical choral databases, wind band resources, and orchestral score collections.
- **Music History & Appreciation OERs:** High-quality, open-license textbooks covering Western classical music, global music cultures, jazz history, and popular music studies.
- **Interactive Ear Training Databases:** Directory of open-source software, web tools, and exercises for chord recognition, sight-singing, and melodic dictation.
