## Supporting Professional Music Instruction

MakeMusic, the creators of MakeMusic Cloud (formerly SmartMusic) and Finale, provides a comprehensive collection of free instructional resources, repertoire advice, and webinars designed to help band, choir, and orchestra directors optimize their teaching practices and integrate technology effectively.

### Key Resources

- **Interactive Repertoire Lists:**Meticulously organized directories of music for Concert Band, String Orchestra, Choir, and Jazz Ensemble, categorized by grade level and state festival lists.
- **Teaching and Technology Webinars:** Professional development videos and guides focused on online teaching strategies, remote student assessments, and modern classroom tools.
- **Grading and Assessment Templates:** Practical rubrics, lesson plans, and assignment configurations designed to streamline evaluation in performance-based ensembles.
- **MakeMusic Blog & Podcasts:** In-depth articles and interviews with leading music education experts, composers, and conductors covering contemporary pedagogical challenges.
