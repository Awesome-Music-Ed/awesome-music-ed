## Centralized Standards & Lesson Alignment for Music Educators

Music Standards Hub is a free educational tool created by Mr. Rob and the team at Prodigies Music to demystify curriculum standards for general music teachers. Compiling over 8,000 standards across all 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C., it empowers educators to quickly look up state requirements, cross-examine frameworks, and verify that their daily lessons meet district and state learning objectives.
