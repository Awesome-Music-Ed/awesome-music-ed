## Summer Programs at Longy

The Longy School of Music of Bard College offers a suite of summer professional development programs designed for music educators seeking to revitalize their teaching, learn new methodologies, and earn graduate credits. With a focus on social change and relevant, modern pedagogical practices, these courses empower educators to reach diverse student populations.

### Key Offerings

- **Modern Band & Culturally Responsive Pedagogy:** Learn how to implement modern band ensembles (guitar, bass, drums, keyboards, vocals) and incorporate popular music into your existing curriculum.
- **Laptop Ensembles & Music Technology:** Discover how to facilitate collaborative music making using laptops, tablets, and digital instruments, creating highly inclusive ensembles for non-traditional music students.
- **Dalcroze Eurhythmics Summer Institute:** Experience the historic eurhythmics methodology of experiencing music through physical movement, ear training, and improvisation.
- **Flexible Graduate Credit:** Courses are available for professional development hours or graduate credits, offering versatile pathways to advance your teaching certification.
