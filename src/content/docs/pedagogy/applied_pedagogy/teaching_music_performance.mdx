*From the publisher:*

## The Ultimate Repertoire and Curriculum Resource for Ensemble Directors

The *Teaching Music through Performance* series is widely considered the absolute cornerstone of instrumental and choral music education literature. Published by GIA Publications and compiled under General Editor Richard Miles, this landmark multi-volume franchise is designed to move ensemble directors beyond basic note-reading toward comprehensive, artistic musical awareness.

Each volume combines masterclass-style chapters written by leading directors and scholars with incredibly thorough, step-by-step Teacher Resource Guides analyzing significant literature across all grade levels.

### Core Pedagogical Features

- **Exhaustive Teacher Resource Guides:** Detailed curriculum guides for hundreds of wind band, orchestral, and choral works. Each guide covers:
  - *Composer Biography & Historical Context:* Background information to share during rehearsals.
  - *Technical & Stylistic Considerations:* Rhythmic, range, and expressive challenges.
  - *Musical Elements & Form:* Comprehensive structural and harmonic breakdowns.
  - *Rehearsal Strategies:* Practical, step-by-step plans to resolve performance hurdles.
- **Expert Pedagogical Essays:** Part I of every volume contains chapters by master educators exploring rehearsal efficiency, score study, programming philosophies, administrative excellence, student leadership, and conductor wellness.
- **Audiophile Companion Recordings:** High-quality recordings (often featuring the North Texas Wind Symphony under Eugene Corporon or other premier ensembles) provide directors and students with a professional sonic benchmark for analyzed works.
- **Expansive Scope:** While it began for Concert Band, the franchise has expanded into essential series for:
  - *Beginning Band & Middle School Band*
  - *Orchestra (String and Full Orchestra)*
  - *Choir (including Middle School Choir)*
  - *Jazz Ensembles*
- **Searchable Database (TeachingMusic.org):** A companion website with an active index database allows directors to search by title, composer, grade level, or volume to quickly find resources for their current programming.

### Pedagogical Value for Music Educators
This series elevates the school rehearsal room into a high-level academic classroom. Rather than simply preparing students for a concert, directors are equipped to teach music theory, history, and aesthetic values directly through their performance literature. It acts as an invaluable guide for undergraduate music education students learning score analysis and a lifelong reference library for veteran educators planning their concert seasons.
