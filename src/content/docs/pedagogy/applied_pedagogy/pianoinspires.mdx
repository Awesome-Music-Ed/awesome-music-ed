## The National Center for Keyboard Pedagogy

Piano Inspires is the digital home of the Frances Clark Center for Keyboard Pedagogy, a leading non-profit organization dedicated to fostering excellence in piano teaching and learning. Through research, professional development events, and a massive database of digital publications, the Center supports keyboard educators at every stage of their career.

### Key Resources

- **Clavier Companion & Archives:** Access a comprehensive library of research articles, teaching tips, historical reviews, and repertoire analyses from premier keyboard pedagogy journals.
- **Teacher Education Courses:** Participate in online professional development courses, webinars, and certification programs designed to refine your studio teaching techniques.
- **Inspiring Piano Repertoire Databases:** Search extensive databases of solo and ensemble piano literature, organized by pedagogical level, genre, and historical era.
- **Inclusion & Accessibility Resources:** Discover specialized resources for teaching piano to students with diverse learning needs, physical differences, or neurodivergence.
