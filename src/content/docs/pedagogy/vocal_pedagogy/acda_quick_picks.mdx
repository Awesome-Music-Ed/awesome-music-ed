*From the American Choral Directors Association website:*

## Standard-Setting Repertoire Choices for Every Choir

The American Choral Directors Association (ACDA) compiles the "Quick Picks" repertoire lists through its dedicated Repertoire & Resources (R&R) committees. These lists provide choral directors with trusted, high-quality repertoire recommendations categorized by difficulty, voicing, and style to ensure artistic growth in rehearsals.

### Key Resources

- **Committees-Curated Selections:** Repertoire recommendations compiled by leading national experts specializing in:
  - **Children's and Community Youth Choirs**
  - **Junior High and Middle School Choirs**
  - **Senior High School Choirs**
  - **College and University Ensembles**
  - **Community and Professional Choirs**
- **Voicing-Specific Categories:** Find outstanding music specifically for SATB, SSAA, TTBB, and two-part or three-part treble ensembles.
- **Multicultural and Historical Collections:** Discover historic sacred works, secular classics, contemporary commissions, and authentic global music traditions with vetted translations.
- **Graded Difficulty Levels:** Easily filter and select music suitable for beginners, intermediate ensembles, or advanced conservatory choirs.
