## Resources for the Modern Voice Studio

The Full Voice is a specialized digital platform providing pedagogical resources, assessment tools, and training materials for voice teachers. Focus areas include young singing voice development, contemporary commercial music (CCM) vocal styles, music theory for singers, and voice studio business management.

### Key Resources

- **Vocal Technique Workbooks:** Meticulously graded vocal exercises, music theory worksheets, and ear training activities designed specifically for young singers (ages 5–18).
- **Contemporary Voice Pedagogy:** Guides on teaching popular music styles, mic technique, belting mechanics safely, and commercial vocal styling.
- **The Full Voice Podcast:** Access professional interviews with leading vocal coaches, ENT doctors, child voice experts, and business consultants.
- **Studio Assessment Tools:** Ready-to-use vocal assessment rubrics, sight-singing cards, and practice planners to keep private voice students motivated.
