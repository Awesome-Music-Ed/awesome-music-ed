*From the Vic Firth website:*

## Definitive Percussion Technique Fundamentals

Percussion 101 is a free, high-quality educational resource designed by Vic Firth to establish a standard of performance and teaching for school percussionists. Led by world-class professional players and educators, the program provides clear video demonstrations and pedagogical worksheets.

### Core Curricular Areas

- **Snare Drum:** Fundamental stick grip (matched and traditional), rebound control, key stroke types, essential rudiments, and rolls.
- **Keyboard Percussion:** Proper mallet selection, stance, stroke mechanics, dynamic control, reading exercises, and double-mallet technique.
- **Timpani:** Pitch tuning mechanics, mallet choice, standard grip, stroke articulation, dampening, and rolls.
- **Concert Accessories:** Specialized instructions for concert bass drum, crash cymbals, triangle, tambourine, and woodblocks, ensuring students play each accessory with professional tone.
- **Printable PDFs & Track Sheets:** Downloadable technique sheets, musical exercises, and performance checklists to easily track student progress.
