## A Modern Guide to Teaching and Playing Brass Instruments

*Brass Techniques and Pedagogy* by Brian N. Weidner is an open-access digital textbook designed specifically for undergraduate brass techniques courses as part of a music education degree, as well as a comprehensive reference guide for active band music educators. 

The text is structured for a one-semester course, written with non-brass playing music education students in mind. Rather than focusing solely on personal performance proficiency, the primary goal of this textbook is to empower future educators to teach beginning and intermediate brass players effectively with sound, diagnostic pedagogy.

### Core Pedagogical Features

- **Flipped Classroom Approach:** The curriculum is designed to allow students to learn technical and pedagogical concepts through home study, reserving valuable classroom time for hands-on teaching, peer-to-peer instruction, and diagnostic practice.
- **Over 60 Integrated Videos:** High-quality, embedded videos provide visual modeling, professional instruction, and guided practice materials for trumpet, horn, trombone, euphonium, and tuba.
- **Comprehensive Teacher Resources:** Includes guided practice sheets, standard warm-up materials, fingering/position charts, instrument troubleshooting guides, and instructional rubrics.
- **Non-Brass Specialist Focus:** Prioritizes developing a broad, expansive understanding of brass fundamentals (embouchure, airflow, articulation, and instrument care) so that directors can quickly diagnose and fix student errors in rehearsal.
