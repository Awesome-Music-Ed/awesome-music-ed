## A Central Resource for Learning, Growth, and Inspiration

The **Black Swamp Percussion Education Hub** is a premier, open-access digital portal established by leading manufacturer Black Swamp Percussion to standardize teaching and performance techniques for concert percussion. Accessing the hub is entirely free, making it an invaluable "plug-and-play" asset for school band directors, private instructors, and percussion students looking to build high-level performance skills.

By featuring university educators and professional orchestral players, the hub demystifies historically neglected concert accessory instruments and snare drum maintenance.

## Percussion 101 Video Series
*For more details, visit: [blackswamp.com/percussion-101](https://www.blackswamp.com/percussion-101)*

Host and Black Swamp Artist **Dr. Trevor Barroero** (Principal Percussionist of the Tucson Symphony Orchestra) leads this foundational video series. It is designed to be a classroom-ready resource for band directors and a rigorous guide for student practice. Each lesson includes clear visual demonstrations, professional performance tips, and downloadable materials:

- **Triangle:** Instruction on striking points for optimal overtone shimmer, holding techniques, and dampening/muffling methods.
- **Tambourine:** Breakdown of thumb rolls, shake rolls, fist-finger techniques, and articulational clarity.
- **Castanets:** Proper hand positioning, developing finger independence, and using castanet machines.
- **Slapstick:** Explains physical swing mechanics and timing precision to execute reliable slapstick impacts in concert repertoire.
- **Woodblocks:** Finding the acoustic "sweet spot" and selecting proper mallets for optimal projection and tone.

## Core Curricular & Resource Areas

- **Deep Dives & Technical Articles:** Practical articles covering snare drum head selection, tension adjustments, tuning snare beds, and mastering specific orchestral excerpts.
- **Artist Lessons:** A growing archive of video lessons and masterclass articles submitted by university percussion professors and symphonic players addressing advanced techniques and rhythm stability.
- **Free Repertoire Downloads:** Downloadable sheet music collections, including intermediate-to-advanced *Soloistic Snare Drum Etudes* by Remi Thornton.
- **Equipment Buying Guides:** Detailed specifications and guides to help school band directors, parents, and boosters select professional-grade concert gear suited for high school and middle school ensembles.
- **Mentorship & Diversity Initiatives:** Panel discussions, career advice, and interviews in partnership with the Alliance for Black Orchestral Percussionists (ABOP).
