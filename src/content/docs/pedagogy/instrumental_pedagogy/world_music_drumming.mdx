## Building Community Through Rhythm

World Music Drumming is a premier percussion and vocal curriculum designed to engage middle school, high school, and elementary general music classrooms. By teaching West African and Latin American rhythmic ensembles in a communal, active setting, it builds ensemble discipline, rhythmic accuracy, and cultural appreciation.

### Core Pedagogical Pillars

- **The Drum Circle & Ensemble Play:** Focuses on standard African and Caribbean drum shapes (congas, djembe, tubanos) along with auxiliary percussion (cowbells, shakeres, woodblocks).
- **Aural & Improvisational Learning:** Prioritizes rote and call-and-response learning, building rhythm memory and physical coordination before sheets of notation are introduced.
- **Vocal & Movement Integration:** Integrates traditional songs, dances, and body percussion alongside drum beats, making musicianship holistic and physical.
- **Social-Emotional Development:** Emphasizes community-building, active listening, teamwork, and personal responsibility as students balance interlocking rhythms across the circle.
