## Interactive Tools for the Modern Music Classroom

Music Room Toolbox is a free, browser-based collection of interactive activities, visual aids, and management utilities designed specifically for K–12 music educators. Engineered for projectors, interactive whiteboards, and student 1:1 devices, it requires no logins or student accounts.
