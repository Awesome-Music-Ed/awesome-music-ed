[My Chord Finder](https://mychordfinder.com/) lets learners select a guitar chord shape or piano chord and compare its displayed notes with playback, without creating an account. Teachers can use its printable capo and transposition tables as references during chord-change and key-change practice; generated shapes still need a comfort check on the student's instrument.
