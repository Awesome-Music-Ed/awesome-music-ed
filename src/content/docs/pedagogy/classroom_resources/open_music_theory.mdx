## The Open-Access Music Theory Textbook

Open Music Theory (OMT) is an open-source, digital textbook designed for college-level music theory courses and high school AP Music Theory. Because it is openly licensed and hosted on GitHub, it provides a highly customizable and interactive resource that is free from commercial licensing costs.

### Key Resources

- **Core Theoretical Concepts:** Comprehensive units covering music fundamentals, diatonic harmony, voice leading, chromatic harmony, and 20th-century post-tonal analysis.
- **Popular and Jazz Theory:** Specialized chapters on lead sheet notation, blues progressions, popular song forms, and jazz harmonic structures.
- **Embedded Audio & Interactive Exercises:** Score examples feature embedded audio playback and self-correcting interactive exercises to help students visualize and hear theoretical concepts.
- **Anthology & Workbook Links:** Connects to an extensive public domain music anthology and collaborative workbooks for practical classroom analysis.
