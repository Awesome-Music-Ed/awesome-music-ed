## Engaging Resources for the Music Classroom

The Music Educator's Toolbox is designed by Carnegie Hall to support elementary and middle school music teachers. By providing comprehensive resources that span grades K–5, this toolbox offers an outstanding array of teaching tools to build core musical capabilities.

### Key Offerings

- **Concept-Focused Lesson Plans:** Access structured lesson units organized around core musical components such as rhythm, pulse, dynamics, pitch, and form.
- **Creative Classroom Worksheets:** Downloadable templates, notation handouts, and composition prompts designed to engage students in active creation.
- **Assessment and Rubric Libraries:** Practical assessment tools to help teachers easily document and grade individual student vocal and instrumental growth.
- **Video & Audio Demonstrations:** High-quality listening recordings and instructional videos that model effective general music teaching strategies.
