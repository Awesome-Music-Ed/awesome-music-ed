*From the Chord Colors website:*

## Explore Music Theory Through Color

In Chord Colors, music theory is interactive, which sparks curiosity. Each of the twelve notes gets a unique color based on the circle of fifths, so students can see harmony. It comes as a reference website with a page for every scale, chord, and progression, plus a free-form web app at `app.chordcolors.com`. There is also a companion freemium iOS app that has identical content to the web app.

### Key Resources

- **Free and Classroom-Safe:** No ads, no accounts, and nothing to install on the website or web app. Ready for a projector, a school Chromebook, or a student's phone.
- **Playable by Touch:** Fully interactive with touches and swipes, so students can strum the guitar, glissando across the piano, or hold full chords with multiple fingers, just like the real thing.
- **A Linkable Page for Every Lesson:** 1,800 scales, 700 chords, and 450 progressions, each on its own page with note formulas, the chords in the key, and piano fingerings for both hands.
- **Play-Along Practice:** A full screen falling-note player where students can explore and play along with strumming, fingerpicking, and piano patterns at any tempo.
