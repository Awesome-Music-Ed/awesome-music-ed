## The Complete Resource for Elementary General Music

MusicplayOnline is an extensive, web-based subscription resource offering a complete general music curriculum. Loaded with thousands of songs, interactive teaching games, solfege models, listening activities, and complete lesson plans, it is a staple of elementary music instruction in schools globally.

### Key Resources

- **Comprehensive Song Library:** Search over 1,000 children's folk songs, international heritage songs, and educational chants with vocal tracks, instrumental tracks, and lyrics videos.
- **Interactive Music Games:** Engaging, game-based reviews for note names, rhythm subdivisions, instrument families, dynamics, and tempo.
- **Concept-Focused Modules:** Dedicated teaching modules for Orff instruments, recorders, ukuleles, solfege, and body percussion.
- **Structured Lesson Planning:** Access complete weekly lesson plans for grades Pre-K through 6, aligned with national standards and easily adaptable for remote or physical classrooms.
