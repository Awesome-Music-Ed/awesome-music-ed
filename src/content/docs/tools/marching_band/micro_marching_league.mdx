## The Online Marching Band Sandbox

Micro Marching League (MML) is a web-based community platform that allows marching arts designers and enthusiasts to build, animate, and share custom micro-sized marching band shows. It provides a unique, highly visual environment for experimenting with drill choreography and show concepts right in your browser.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Browser-Based Drill Creator">
		Design custom marching drill, transitions, and field patterns directly in any modern browser without installing heavy software.
	</Card>
	<Card title="3D Visualizer & Playback">
		Watch your custom shows perform in a real-time, animated 3D visualizer that plays back movements alongside uploaded audio tracks.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Community Sharing & Leagues">
		Share your drill designs with a global community of marching enthusiasts, participate in design contests, and review others' work.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Choreography Sandbox">
		Experiment with body movements, instrument angles, and color guard equipment spins to test visual concepts before taking them to the field.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
