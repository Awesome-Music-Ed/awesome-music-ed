*From the Pygraphics website:*

## The World Leader in Marching Band Software

Pyware 3D is widely recognized as the most powerful and comprehensive drill design software in the marching arts. Used by high schools, universities, drum corps, and major events like the Super Bowl halftime shows, Pyware provides unmatched tools for drawing, animating, and charting complex field choreography.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Advanced 3D Animation & Simulation">
		Visualize your show on any field or arena with hyper-realistic 3D performers, custom uniform skins, equipment visuals, and instrument models.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Robust Drawing & Transition Tools">
		Create flawless circles, blocks, lines, and complex morphing shapes with automated transition paths and step-reduction algorithms.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Interactive Audio Syncing">
		Link your drill sheets directly to show recordings or MIDI files, allowing you to synchronize steps and counts perfectly to the music.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Collision Detection & Safety">
		Ensure safety on the field with built-in speed and stride analyzers that warn you when pathways collide or exceed athletic limits.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Seamless Mobile Sync (UDB Integration)">
		Export drill packages directly to mobile applications such as UDBapp for seamless distribution to students and field staff.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Comprehensive Printing & Coordinate Sheets">
		Generate customizable student coordinate printouts, director field books, and detailed master sheets for smooth rehearsals.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
