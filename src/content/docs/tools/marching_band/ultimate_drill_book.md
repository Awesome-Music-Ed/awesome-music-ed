## Interactive Field Navigation for the Modern Marching Arts

Ultimate Drill Book (UDB) has revolutionized how marching bands, drum corps, and indoor ensembles learn and rehearse drill. Through the UDBapp, performers and directors have instant access to interactive drill charts, real-time coordinate sheets, animations, and music—all in the palm of their hands.

### Core Features

- **Interactive Animation Engine:** Performers can watch their individual paths and dot-to-dot animations across the field, allowing them to see exactly how their movement fits into the ensemble's shape.
- **Customized Coordinate Sheets:** View customized coordinate instructions for every single set, tailored specifically to the individual performer's spot.
- **UDB Draw & Notes:** Draw pathways directly on the screen, write rehearsal notes, and highlight critical instructions for specific sets.
- **Director's View:** Directors and instructors can track student attendance, assign sets, view every performer's position, and make real-time corrections on the field.
- **Offline Synchronization:** Sync show drill charts while on Wi-Fi and access all features smoothly on the rehearsal field without requiring an active cellular connection.
- **Split-Screen Music Integration:** View sheet music alongside drill coordinates, enabling performers to learn their drill and musical checkpoints simultaneously.
