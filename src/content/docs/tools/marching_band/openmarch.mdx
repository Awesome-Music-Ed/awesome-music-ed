## Free and Open-Source Drill Design for Everyone

OpenMarch is a community-driven, open-source software project aiming to provide a powerful, modern, and accessible alternative for marching band drill designers. With cross-platform compatibility and robust vector-based editing tools, OpenMarch empowers independent designers and band directors to draft high-quality drill without financial barriers.
