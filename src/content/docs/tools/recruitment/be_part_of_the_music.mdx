Be Part of the Music is a free, comprehensive recruitment and retention advocacy platform designed to help K-12 music educators build and maintain strong music programs. It provides pre-made materials, roadmaps, and automation tools to simplify the student enrollment process.

## Core Features

- **Director Dashboard**: Access a collection of daily ideas, calendars, templates, and strategies designed for music program recruitment and community engagement.
- **Customized Program Webpages**: Set up custom recruiting landing pages containing instrument demonstration videos and online fitting assessments.
- **Step-by-Step Roadmaps**: Follow sequential checklists and task lists outlining timelines, letters to parents, and promotional calendars.
- **Recruitment Videos**: Use high-quality instrument demonstrations and promotional videos to present the band, orchestra, and choral experience to incoming students.
