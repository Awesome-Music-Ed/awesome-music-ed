## Transcribe, Scan and Practice in One Workspace

MusiCraft AI is a browser based music and audio workspace guided by an AI agent. For music students and teachers, it turns recordings into lead sheets with melody, chords and tempo, scans printed sheet music into a playable score, and separates songs into individual parts for practice.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Audio to Sheet Music">
		Transcribe a recording into a lead sheet with melody, chords and tempo, or export it as MIDI or MusicXML for use in notation software.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Sheet Music Scanner">
		Scan printed sheet music from a photo or PDF and convert it into a playable digital score, MIDI or MusicXML.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Stem Splitter for Rehearsal">
		Split any song into stems across 66 modes, including SATB choir parts, drum kit pieces and single instruments, so students can practice along with one part.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Free Trial">
		Runs in a desktop or mobile browser with a free trial available.
	</Card>
