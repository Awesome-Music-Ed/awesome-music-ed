## The Free, Award-Winning Educational Studio

BandLab for Education is a secure, cloud-based music creation platform that connects students with the power of modern music-making. It works on Chromebooks, iPads, phones, and PCs, providing an accessible and collaborative workspace for students of all backgrounds.
