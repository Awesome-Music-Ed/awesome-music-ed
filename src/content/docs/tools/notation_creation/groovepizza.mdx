*From the NYU Music Experience Design Lab website:*

## A Slice of Creative Rhythm

Groove Pizza is a circular, grid-based rhythm generator and sequencer designed to make creative beatmaking accessible, intuitive, and fun. It connects rhythm, math, and geometry, allowing students to explore drum patterns visually and conceptually.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Circular Visual Sequencer">
		Visualize rhythms as geometric shapes inside a circle (pizza slices), making the mathematical and structural nature of rhythm immediately apparent.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Multi-Genre Sound Kits">
		Switch instantly between classic 808 drum kits, acoustic drums, Latin percussion, and electronic synth beats.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Rhythm Math & Polyrhythms">
		Explore subdivision, syncopation, and polyrhythms visually by toggling different slice amounts (e.g. 12 or 16 beats).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Direct MIDI & Soundtrap Export">
		Export your grooves directly to Soundtrap, download them as MIDI files, or share your custom creation as a short link.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Classroom Friendly Design">
		Requires no login or setup. It works on Chromebooks, tablets, and phones, making it a stellar warm-up tool for elementary and middle school classrooms.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
