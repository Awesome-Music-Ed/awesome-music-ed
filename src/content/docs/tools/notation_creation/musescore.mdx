*From the MuseScore website:*

## Create, Play Back, and Print Beautiful Sheet Music

MuseScore is the world's most popular open-source music notation software. It is completely free, runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux, and offers a professional-grade feature set that rivals expensive proprietary alternatives.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Free and Open Source">
		No licensing costs, subscriptions, or feature limits. Perfect for schools looking to provide notation software to every student.
	</Card>
	<Card title="MuseScore 4 Engine & Muse Sounds">
		Features a completely redesigned engraving engine and the stunning Muse Sounds sample libraries for professional-quality playback.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Intuitive & Fast Note Input">
		Enter notes using computer keyboards, MIDI controllers, or on-screen tools with a user-friendly, modern interface.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Massive Community Sheet Music Library">
		Access and share thousands of community-uploaded arrangements and transcriptions on MuseScore.com.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flexible Import/Export Formats">
		Easily import and export MusicXML, MIDI, PDF, and audio files (WAV, MP3, OGG) for maximum interoperability.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Advanced Layout and Engraving">
		Complete control over staff spacing, vertical casting, fonts, and parts generation for high-quality printing.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
