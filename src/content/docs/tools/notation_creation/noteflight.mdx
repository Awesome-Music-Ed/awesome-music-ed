*From the Noteflight website:*

## The Online Music Notation and Learning Community

Noteflight is a powerful, web-based notation platform that lets you create, view, print, and hear professional music notation on any device. With the backing of Hal Leonard, Noteflight also provides an extensive library of popular digital sheet music that teachers can easily adapt for their ensembles.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Noteflight Learn Integration">
		Provides a private, secure site for your school where students can create music, record performances, and receive teacher feedback.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Hal Leonard Sheet Music Library">
		Access thousands of digital scores from Hal Leonard's catalog for performance, analysis, and student practice.
	</Card>
	<Card title="SoundCheck Performance Assessment">

	</Card>
	<Card title="Collaborative Music Sharing">

		Plays back music beautifully in any browser using high-quality virtual instrument sounds with no plug-ins required.
	</Card>
	<Card title="LMS Syncing">
		Integrates perfectly with Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology, and other major educational portals.
