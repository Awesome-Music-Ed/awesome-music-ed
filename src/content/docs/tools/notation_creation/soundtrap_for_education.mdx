*From the Soundtrap website:*

## Empower Every Student's Voice

Soundtrap for Education is a collaborative, cloud-based digital audio workstation (DAW) that works on any device. Students and teachers can co-create music, record podcasts, and edit audio in real-time, making it an ideal tool for modern classrooms and virtual collaboration.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Real-Time Collaboration">
		Enables students to work together on the same project from different devices or classrooms, much like a collaborative document editor.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Classroom & LMS Integration">
		Integrates seamlessly with Google Classroom, Canvas, Schoology, and Microsoft Teams to make assignments and grading simple.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Intuitive Digital Audio Workstation">
		Features a user-friendly multi-track recording editor, virtual instrument keyboard, loops, and a built-in software synthesizer.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Safe & Secure Environment">
		COPPA and FERPA compliant, providing a secure online sandbox where students only interact within your controlled school domain.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Podcast Recording & Editing">
		Includes specialized features for podcast creators, such as automated transcripts, interactive text editing, and voice enhancers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Built-In Loop Library">
		Access thousands of high-quality loops, beats, and sound effects across multiple genres to quickly spark student creativity.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
