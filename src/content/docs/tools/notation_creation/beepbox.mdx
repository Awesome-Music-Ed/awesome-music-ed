## Sketch and Share Chiptune Melodies Instantly

BeepBox is a free, web-based tool for sketching and sharing instrumental melodies, specializing in retro, chiptune, and 8-bit synthetic sounds. Its entirely graphical, grid-based approach makes it incredibly accessible for students to explore melody, harmony, and song structure.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Completely Free & No Sign-Up">
		Start writing music immediately in your browser. All song data is saved directly inside the page URL, making sharing simple.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Grid-Based Sequencer">
		Simply click on a grid to place notes. Students do not need to know traditional notation to start composing melodies and chords.
	</Card>
	<Card title="8-Bit Synthesizer Engine">
		Explore retro wave shapes (square, triangle, saw), noise generators, and simple FM synthesizers to customize your instrument tracks.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flexible Export Formats">
		Export your finished compositions as MIDI files, WAV/MP3 audio, JSON data, or short, shareable links.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Multi-Channel Sequencing">
		Create rich arrangements with up to four simultaneous channels (pitch tracks and drum noise tracks).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Offline Capability">
		Can be downloaded and run completely offline, making it highly reliable for classrooms with intermittent internet.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
