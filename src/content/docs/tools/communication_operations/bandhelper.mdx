*From the BandHelper website:*

## The Live Performance and Band Management Portal

BandHelper keeps your band organized on and off the stage. From scheduling rehearsals to automating your stage settings, BandHelper centralizes all your critical band information in one cloud-based system accessible by all band members.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Setlist & Chord Chart Management">
		Build custom setlists, attach scrollable lyrics and chord charts, and share them instantly with all members of the ensemble.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Scheduling & Event Coordination">
		Track gig details, rehearsal schedules, soundcheck times, and member availability in a centralized calendar.
	</Card>
	<Card title="MIDI & Automation Control">
		Automate your live performance by sending MIDI program changes to your keyboard, processor, or lighting rig directly from the setlist.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Stage Layouts & Tech Riders">
		Design and share stage plots, input lists, and technical riders with venue sound engineers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Shared Band Finance Tracker">
		Manage band income, track expenses, record gig payouts, and calculate individual member shares transparently.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Offline Sync & Performance Mode">
		Sync data to your mobile devices for offline access, and use the dedicated performance screen with auto-scroll and metronome sync.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
