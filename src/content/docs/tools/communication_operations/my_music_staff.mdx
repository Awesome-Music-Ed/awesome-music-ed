*From the My Music Staff website:*

## The All-in-One Studio Management Software

My Music Staff is designed to take the administrative headache out of running your private music studio. Whether you are a solo teacher or managing a multi-teacher school, My Music Staff streamlines your daily tasks so you can focus on teaching.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Scheduling & Calendar">
		Manage lesson schedules, teacher availability, and student attendance in a unified, interactive calendar with automated reminders.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flexible Invoicing & Billing">
		Generate professional invoices, manage recurring tuition, track expenses, and accept online payments via credit card or auto-pay.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Student & Parent Portal">
		Provide individual portals where students and parents can view schedules, log practice times, download files, and track lesson history.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Practice Log & Tracker">
		Encourage student accountability with built-in practice timers, goal setting, and real-time teacher feedback loops.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Multi-Teacher Support">
		Scale your studio with granular permissions, payroll tracking, and easy scheduling across multiple instructors.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Resource Library">
		Share sheet music, MP3 recordings, and learning materials securely with specific students or entire classes.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
