CutTime is an administrative management platform built specifically for K-12 and collegiate fine arts programs, including bands, orchestras, choirs, guard, and theater departments. It is designed to streamline day-to-day operations, communication, and inventory tracking, allowing educators to focus on teaching.

## Key Features

- **Inventory Tracking**: Manage and assign instruments, uniforms, sheet music, and equipment using built-in barcode and QR scanning.
- **Communication & Magic Links**: Send announcements, reminders, and updates directly to parent and student mobile devices without requiring traditional app installations or logins.
- **Financial Management**: Track school fees, uniform deposits, and payments, with integrated options for online billing.
- **Student & Parent Database**: Centralize contact information, ensemble assignments, and parent volunteer rosters securely.
