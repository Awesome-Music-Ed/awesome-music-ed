Trello is a project management tool that can be used for a variety of purposes. It provides a visual way to organize tasks and and collaborate on projects. Trello comes as a web app, desktop app, and mobile app.

---

*From the Trello website:*

# Your productivity powerhouse

Trello is the ultimate tool for getting things done. Whether you're managing daily to-dos, setting personal goals, or organizing your life, Trello helps you focus on what matters most and always stay on top of your game.

Stay organized and efficient with Inbox, Boards, and Planner. Every to-do, idea, or responsibility—no matter how small—finds its place, keeping you at the top of your game.

Trello cards keep all of your important information organized and accessible in one place. Add due dates, attach files, and mark to-do’s as “Done” when completed. Plus, AI helps you refine your text for a polished finish!

### Due dates
Due dates go hand-in-hand with staying organized. In Trello, they’re easy to set, hard to miss (with reminders!), and oh-so-satisfying to mark as “done.”

### Attachments
No more digging through endless email chains to find attachments. Just drag and drop them onto a card so the right files stay with the right tasks.

### Checklists
Your best tool to overpower overwhelming asks. Break big tasks into small ones, check things off the list, and watch that status bar go to 100% complete.
