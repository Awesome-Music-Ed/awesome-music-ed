Remind is a widely adopted communication platform designed for K-12 education that enables teachers, administrators, and directors to send safe, real-time messages to students, parents, and school communities. It supports two-way messaging, mass announcements, and document sharing while keeping personal phone numbers completely private.

## Key Features

- **Safe Mobile Messaging**: Reach students and parents directly via SMS text messages, push notifications, or email without disclosing personal phone numbers.
- **Ensemble-Based Classes**: Create specific groups for separate bands, choirs, orchestras, or sections to send targeted announcements.
- **Two-Way or Broadcast-Only**: Configure classes to allow parent/student replies or restrict communication to one-way notifications only.
- **Translation Services**: Automatically translate messages into over 90 languages, helping to engage diverse parent communities.
- **Safe Archiving**: Maintain a complete, un-deletable history of all communications for school administrative compliance and safety records.
- **Document & Media Sharing**: Distribute rehearsal schedules, sheet music attachments, permission slips, and audio files directly through messages.
