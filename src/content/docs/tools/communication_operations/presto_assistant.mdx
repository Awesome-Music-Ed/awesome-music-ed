Presto Assistant is a web-based administrative management platform designed for school music, theater, and dance programs. It helps educators manage the logistical, organizational, and financial aspects of running a performing arts department.

## Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Centralized Communication">
        Facilitates mass communication to students and parents via email and text, organized by ensemble or class section.
	</Card>
    <Card title="Unified Parent Portal">
        Enables parents to manage information, view fees, and submit forms for multiple children from a single login interface.
	</Card>
    <Card title="Inventory Management">
        Tracks instruments, equipment, uniforms, sheet music library accessions, and assignments systematically.
	</Card>
    <Card title="Financial Tracking">
        Records student balances, fee schedules, and incoming payments, with support for credit card payments through third-party integrations (Stripe, Vanco).
	</Card>
    <Card title="Digital Forms">
        Allows directors to create, distribute, and collect standard field trip forms, handbooks, and policy agreements electronically.
	</Card>
    <Card title="Roster & Section Assignments">
        Manages class assignments, student leadership roles, and section details in one shared database.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
