## Elegant Drag-and-Drop Building for Music Programs

**Weebly** (owned by Square) is a classic, highly accessible website builder favored by music educators, booster organizations, and private studio teachers who want a professional, creative design without needing any coding skills. Its intuitive, visual drag-and-drop builder allows you to construct a full-featured website in hours.

With built-in e-commerce tools (powered by Square), Weebly is particularly useful for booster clubs that need to collect student fees, sell merchandise, or take program donations directly online.

### Key Features

### Music Education Use Cases

- **Private Lesson Studio Portals:** Share your lesson policies, studio bios, and embed scheduling widgets (such as Acuity or Calendly) to attract and coordinate new students.
- **Booster Club Storefronts:** Take credit card payments for concert shirts, band camp fees, or instrument rental fees without needing a separate payment portal.
- **Ensemble Media Galleries:** Showcase high-quality audio files, performance videos, and photo galleries from recent concert tours or marching band competitions.
- **Alumni Directories:** Keep in touch with program graduates by hosting registration forms, donation drives, and event announcements.
