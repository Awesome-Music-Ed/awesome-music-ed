## The Free Collaborative Hub for School Music Programs

**Google Sites** is an exceptionally popular website builder among K-12 music educators and collegiate directors. Since it is included at no additional cost in the standard Google Workspace (including Google Workspace for Education), it provides a secure, easy-to-manage web presence without any hosting fees or complex design requirements. 

For band, choir, and orchestra directors, Google Sites is the ideal platform for building a "Program Hub" or "Student Portal" to house handbooks, field trip forms, audition results, and rehearsal files.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Workspace Integration">
		Embed items directly from Google Drive, including Google Docs (handbooks), Sheets (audition results), Slides, and Google Calendars for performance dates.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Classroom Collaboration">
		Share editing permissions easily with student leaders, booster board members, or co-directors to keep the site updated collectively.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Zero Cost & Security">
		No web hosting fees or domain requirements. Permissions can be locked so only students signed into school district Google accounts can access resources.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Mobile Friendly Layouts">
		Designs are automatically responsive, meaning parents and students can easily view rehearsal schedules and announcements on their phones.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

### Music Education Use Cases

- **Digital Band/Orchestra Handbooks:** Replace printed booklets with an interactive site page where parents can read policies and submit an embedded Google Form confirmation.
- **Audition Portals:** Post scale requirements, audition excerpts, and final ensemble rosters securely.
- **Section Leader Pages:** Create specific sub-pages managed by student leaders to post section goals, warm-up exercises, and section announcements.
- **Booster Club Hub:** Dedicated space for parent boosters to manage volunteer sign-ups (embedded via Google Sheets or third-party widgets) and fundraising information.
