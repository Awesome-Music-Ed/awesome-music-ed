Canva for Education is an all-in-one visual communication platform designed specifically for the classroom. It provides K-12 teachers and students with free access to the premium features typically found in a paid Canva Pro account, plus specialized tools for educational management.

## Who Can Join?

* Primary/Secondary Teachers: Must be currently active and certified in a formally accredited K-12 school.
* School Support Staff: Includes librarians, curriculum specialists, and learning support assistants.
* K-12 Students: Gain access only through an invitation from their teacher.
* Districts: Entire school systems can roll out access via [Canva for Districts](https://www.canva.com/education/schools/) using Single Sign-On (SSO).

## Get Started

   1. Sign Up: Visit the [Canva for Education](https://www.canva.com/education/) page and select "Get Verified".
   2. Verify Status: Use your school email address for instant verification. If not recognized, you may need to upload a teaching license or proof of employment.
   3. Invite Students: Once verified, you can create a "Class" and invite students via email, a shared link, or by importing your Google Classroom roster.
