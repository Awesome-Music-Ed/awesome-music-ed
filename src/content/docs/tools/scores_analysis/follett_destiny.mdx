## Centralize School Asset and Library Tracking

Follett Destiny Library Manager is the leading library management system designed to connect students and educators to school resources. In music departments, Destiny is utilized to catalog vast physical sheet music archives, track instrument inventories, and manage uniform distributions with barcode tracking.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Barcode Asset Tracking">
		Assign barcodes to physical choral octavos, band scores, and instruments to streamline the check-out and check-in process.
	</Card>
	<Card title="MARC Cataloguing Standards">
		Maintain a highly searchable catalog of music titles, composers, and instrumentation using standard library metadata formats.
	</Card>
	<Card title="District-Wide Inventory Management">
		Track resources across multiple school sites, allowing high school and middle school music programs to share sheet music catalogs.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Fine & Lost Item Tracking">
		Automate notifications for missing music folders or overdue instruments, maintaining high accountability for school assets.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
