## The Virtual Choral Library

**Musica International** is a comprehensive, multilingual, and collaborative database dedicated to the world’s choral repertoire. Acting as an essential tool for choir directors, conductors, musicologists, and music educators, it catalogues extensive details for more than 200,000 choral scores globally. 

Through its massive database, Musica International helps choral directors discover new repertoire, study historical contexts, master foreign language pronunciations, and connect directly with publishers.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Massive Global Archive">
		Explore a database of over 200,000 detailed entries representing centuries of choral works, folk songs, oratorios, and contemporary choral literature.

		Filter repertoire using up to 100 distinct criteria, including voicing (SATB, SSAA, etc.), level of difficulty, language, century, instrumentation, and performance theme.

	<Card title="Pronunciations & Translations">
		Access accurate, high-quality translation texts in English, French, German, and Spanish, along with phonetic pronunciation guides.

	<Card title="Multimedia & Publisher Connections">
		Access composer biographies, listen to audio/video recordings, and find direct links to publishers to purchase sheet music.
	</Card>

### Collaborative Community
Musica International is maintained by a non-profit association supported by a global network of volunteers, choirs, and national federations. 

* **IFCM Partnership:** Members of the *International Federation for Choral Music* (IFCM) receive full, unrestricted, privileged access to the complete database free of charge via the IFCM portal.
