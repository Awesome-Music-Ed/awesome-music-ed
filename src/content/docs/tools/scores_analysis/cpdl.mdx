## The ChoralWiki Home

The Choral Public Domain Library (CPDL) is one of the largest web-based portals for free choral sheet music. Operating as a wiki database (ChoralWiki), it hosts a vast array of choral scores, texts, translations, and rehearsal tracks compiled by choral musicians globally.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Dedicated Choral Archive">
		Find thousands of scores specifically curated for SATB, SSAA, TTBB, madrigals, motets, masses, and contemporary choral works.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Lyric Translations & IPA Guides">
		Access comprehensive lyric translations, liturgical texts, and International Phonetic Alphabet (IPA) pronunciation guides.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Free Rehearsal Audio Files">
		Download MIDI, MP3, and practice tracks to help singers learn individual parts (Soprano, Alto, Tenor, Bass) at home.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flexible Scoring & Key Searches">
		Search scores by specific vocal voicing, composer, era, language, or liturgical season to find the perfect piece for your choir.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
