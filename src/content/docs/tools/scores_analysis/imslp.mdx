## The Petrucci Music Library

IMSLP, also known as the Petrucci Music Library, is a community-driven digital archive of public domain music scores, as well as performances and recordings by contemporary artists who wish to share their music freely. It is the largest and most comprehensive repository of sheet music on the internet.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Massive Public Domain Catalog">
		Access hundreds of thousands of scores, parts, and historical editions of classical music from thousands of composers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Free PDF Downloads">
		Download scores, instrumental parts, and study transcripts directly as standard PDF files for printing and digital music readers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Historic Audio Recordings">
		Listen to thousands of classical recordings, historical performances, and audio files synced directly to the sheet music.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Collaborative Wiki Platform">
		Maintained by a global community of musicologists, librarians, and music students ensuring accurate metadata and copyright verification.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Composer Profiles & Works Lists">
		Explore comprehensive works lists, composition dates, performance histories, and instrumentations for classical composers.
	</Card>
