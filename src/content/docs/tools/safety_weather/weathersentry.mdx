## Keep Your Performers Safe on the Field

WeatherSentry Sports provides professional weather decision support to athletic directors, event organizers, and marching band directors. By providing real-time alerts on lightning strikes and tracking Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT), WeatherSentry helps schools stay compliant with safety regulations and prevent heat illness during demanding outdoor rehearsals.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) Monitoring">
		Track heat, humidity, wind, and solar radiation levels to accurately calculate environmental heat stress on marching band students.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Customizable Lightning Alerts">
		Receive automated SMS or email alerts when lightning is detected within specified radii (e.g. 8-mile or 10-mile boundaries) of your location.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Professional Meteorological Forecasts">
		Access hourly wind, rain, and temperature outlooks designed specifically for stadium events and field rehearsals.
	</Card>
	<Card title="All-Clear Rehearsal Notifications">
		Receive clear "all-clear" countdown timers so directors know exactly when it is safe to return to the field after a severe weather disruption.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
