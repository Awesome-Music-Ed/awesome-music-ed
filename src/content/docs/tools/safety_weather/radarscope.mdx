## Professional-Grade Weather Radar for Your Phone

RadarScope is a specialized display utility for weather enthusiasts and professionals that allows you to view NEXRAD Level 3 and super-resolution radar data along with tornadoes, severe thunderstorms, flash floods, and special marine warnings. It is an indispensable tool for outdoor marching band rehearsals and stadium events.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="High-Resolution Reflectivity">
		View super-resolution reflectivity data to precisely track the intensity, velocity, and boundary lines of approaching storms.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Real-Time Lightning Tracking">
		Monitor lightning strikes in the immediate vicinity to safely execute outdoor rehearsals and determine when to clear the field.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Severe Weather Warnings">
		Receive instant alerts for National Weather Service warnings, including severe thunderstorms, flash floods, and tornadoes.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Precise GPS Location Overlay">
		Overlay your exact location on the radar grid to see precisely how far a storm is from your rehearsal field or stadium.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
