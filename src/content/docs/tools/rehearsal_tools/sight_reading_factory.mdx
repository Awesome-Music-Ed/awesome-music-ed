## Unlimited Sight-Reading Exercises, Customized for Your Needs

Sight Reading Factory makes practicing sight-reading easy and effective. Because it generates brand-new exercises on demand based on your precise constraints, students never memorize pieces. It is the premier resource for ensembles, studio instructors, and state music assessments.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Instant Custom Generation">
		Select your instrument, difficulty level, key signature, time signature, rhythm constraints, and ranges to generate a unique exercise in seconds.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Full Ensemble Formatting">
		Generate multi-part, harmonized sight-reading exercises for concert bands, string orchestras, and choral ensembles with transpositions handled automatically.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Interactive Playback & Cursor">
		Hear how the exercise sounds with realistic synthesized playback, or practice along with a visual metronome and cursor.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Student Assessments & Grading">
		Integrate with your LMS to assign specific sight-reading exercises, receive student audio submissions, and grade them directly.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Standard Alignment & Levels">
		Uses a highly calibrated, 10-level progressive system that aligns with state and national music standards.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Unlimited Sheet Music Printing">
		Generate, display on a projector, or print out physical sheets of music for classroom use instantly.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
