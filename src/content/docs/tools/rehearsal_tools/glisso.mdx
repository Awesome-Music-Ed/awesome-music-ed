## Modern, Gamified Practice for Band and Orchestra

Glisso is a free, web-based music practice application engineered to drill the fundamental skills that benefit most from short, daily repetition—including sight-reading, note-by-note scales, rhythm tapping, and instrument fingerings. Designed specifically for instrumental music programs, it works on any browser without downloads or specialized equipment, providing acoustic pitch detection through the microphone as well as silent fingerings.

### Core Instrument & Practice Capabilities

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Silent Brass Fingering Drills">
		Brass players (trumpet, French horn, trombone, euphonium, and tuba) can drill valve combinations and slide positions silently using keyboard number keys or mobile touchscreens.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Real-Time Audio & Pitch Assessment">
		Listens via device microphone to instantly analyze pitch accuracy and rhythmic precision for woodwinds, strings, brass, and percussion against a steady metronome.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Form-First Sight-Reading">
		Generates musically coherent sight-reading passages with natural phrase structures, pinned keys, and balanced intervals rather than purely random note sequences.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Daily Challenge & Streaks">
		Offers a unified daily sight-reading excerpt shared across the community with built-in practice streak tracking, motivational statistics, and healthy competition.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Rhythm Tapping & Holding">
		Drill complex syncopations, dotted figures, and subdivisions without an instrument by tapping or holding spacebar/touch controls in time with the beat.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Custom MusicXML & MIDI Import">
		Import school concert repertoire or custom method exercises to create guided play-along tracks, custom tempo controls, and targeted section assignments.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
