*From the Sonic Visualiser website:*

## Visualise, Analyse, and Annotate Your Audio Files

Sonic Visualiser is designed to be the first program you reach for when you want to look at a musical audio file rather than just listen to it. Highly popular in academic musicology and performance research, it provides state-of-the-art spectrogram and waveform visualization.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="High-Resolution Waveform & Spectrograms">
		View audio files in extreme detail, checking amplitude over time and analyzing frequency distributions in real-time.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Flexible Visual Layers">
		Stack multiple spectrograms, waveforms, and annotation layers on top of each other to compare audio features.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Audio Annotation & Labeling">
		Add custom labels, time stamps, and regions to highlight specific sections of a piece for classroom analysis.
	</Card>
	<Card title="VAMP Plugin Compatibility">
		Integrate third-party plugins to automatically estimate tempo, pitch tracks, chords, note onset, and tonal keys.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Time-Stretched Playback">
		Slow down audio playback and loop segments to study intricate acoustic textures or fast improvisations without loss of quality.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
