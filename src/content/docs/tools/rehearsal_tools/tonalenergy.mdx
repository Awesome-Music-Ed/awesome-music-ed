## The Ultimate Practice Companion

TonalEnergy is an all-in-one tuner, metronome, and recording application designed for musicians of all levels. Known for its iconic green smiley-face target feedback, TonalEnergy helps performers develop a keen ear for pitch, a strong sense of rhythm, and deep insight into tone quality.

### Key Features

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Target-Based Intonation Feedback">
		Features the famous interactive green face that lights up when you hit the center of your target pitch, making intonation visual and engaging for students.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Multi-Octave Sound Generator">
		Generate pure reference tones, customizable chords, and auto-tuning drones using high-quality internal instrument samples.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Advanced Metronome Engine">
		Highly customizable metronome with multiple subdivisions, voice counts, accent patterns, and programmable tempo changes.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Visual Waveform & Spectral Analysis">
		Inspect tone shape, envelope, harmonic overtones, and amplitude fluctuations in real-time with high-resolution visualizers.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Built-In Audio & MIDI Recording">
		Record rehearsals or practice sessions, slow down playback speed without changing pitch, and export files easily for assessment.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Temperament & Pitch Calibration">
		Supports historical temperaments, equal temperament, and dynamic just intonation tuning for full ensemble tuning calibration.
	</Card>
