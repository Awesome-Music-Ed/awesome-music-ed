## A Free Guitar Reference and Practice Suite

Open Guitar Chords is a free, browser-based reference for guitar students and teachers. It includes chord diagrams for 172 chord shapes, with fingering, notes, and audio playback, plus a set of practice tools that run without an account or installation.

The reference covers common open, barre, and jazz voicings. The tools section includes a chromatic tuner, a metronome, a chord identifier, a scale finder, and a fretboard trainer. The site is available in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese.

### Classroom Use Cases

- **Beginning guitar classes:** Students can look up chord fingerings and hear the chord without installing an app.
- **Independent practice:** The tuner, metronome, and fretboard trainer support routine practice at home.
- **Theory reinforcement:** Chord pages show the notes in each shape, and the scale finder and chord identifier help connect shapes to theory.

### Limitations

The site is a reference and practice tool, not a sequential curriculum. It does not include video lessons, assignments, or progress tracking.
