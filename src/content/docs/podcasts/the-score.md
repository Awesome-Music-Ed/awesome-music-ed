## About

The Score is an Urban Music Education Podcast hosted by Eric and Justin. They provide tips and strategies through honest discussions about their experience teaching music in an Urban setting. The goal is to provide a positive and solution-based narrative to create more effective, compassionate and culturally relevant music educators.

The Score is part of the [Revival Music Project](https://www.revivalmusicproject.org/), which partners with school districts, universities, and educators to deliver innovative, culturally responsive, and compassionate music education solutions.
