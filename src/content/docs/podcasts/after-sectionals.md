## About

The podcast about all things band. After Sectionals comes to you from the Stiles MS band hall - home of chickens and lost tuners. Join Darcy Vogt Williams, Jenna Yee, and TJ West as they talk band pedagogy, talk smack, and everything in between right after sectionals.
