## Music Technology and Productivity for Educators

Music Ed Tech Talk is a podcast and blog hosted by Robby Burns, exploring the intersection of music education, technology, and personal productivity. Featuring guest interviews with leading developers, composers, and tech-savvy educators, the show offers highly practical advice on integrating software, tablets, and automation tools into daily teaching and studio workflows.

### Key Focus Areas

- **Tablet Workflows & Digital Sheet Music:** Deep-dives into apps like forScore and Newzik to organize, annotate, and perform music digitally.
- **Productivity & Studio Administration:** Practical tips for using task managers (OmniFocus, Todoist), note-taking apps, and automations to streamline grading and email communications.
- **Notation & DAW Tutorials:** Guides on using modern engraving software (Dorico, Sibelius) and cloud-based collaborative DAWs (Soundtrap, BandLab) in classroom instruction.
- **Hardware & Gear Reviews:** Practical recommendations for microphones, speakers, audio interfaces, and digital instruments suitable for school music labs.
